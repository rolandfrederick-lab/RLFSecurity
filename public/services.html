Services

Security services for metro Detroit

Every engagement starts with a site assessment. From there we build a post schedule, officer profile and reporting plan that fits your facility, not a template.

01

Armed & Unarmed Guard Services

Uniformed officers for fixed posts, lobbies, access control and after-hours coverage. Armed officers are licensed and qualified per Michigan requirements.

02

Healthcare & Crisis Facility Security

Officers trained for behavioral-health and crisis settings: de-escalation, patient dignity, controlled access and coordination with clinical staff.

03

Event Security

Crowd management, entry screening and perimeter coverage for private, community and church events of any size.

04

Mobile Patrol

Scheduled and randomized patrols for properties that need a visible presence without a full-time post. Logged and reported.

05

Site Risk Assessments

A walk-through of your building's layout, entry points, lighting and procedures, delivered as a written report with prioritized recommendations.

06

Security Program Consulting

Post orders, staffing plans, incident reporting and emergency procedures for new facilities or programs that need to be built from the ground up.
